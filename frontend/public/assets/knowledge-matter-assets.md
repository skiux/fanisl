- Mode: `stylized-concept`
- Use: the dispersed source-material state at the beginning of the journey
- Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: cinematic 16:9 background asset for a premium financial knowledge-engine website

> Primary request: Create an original abstract physical sculpture that represents scattered source material condensing into durable knowledge. A flowing formation of hundreds of paper-thin translucent vellum layers, archival microfilm strips, frosted mineral sheets, delicate sage and copper indexing filaments, and small warm luminous nodes. The formation should feel like a sophisticated scientific specimen and a living archive, not a generic technology render.
>
> Scene/backdrop: warm ivory atmospheric space with subtle depth haze; the sculpture rises from the lower center and opens toward the right, with generous quiet negative space in the upper-left and center-left for interface typography.
>
> Style/medium: high-end cinematic CGI, macro material photography, editorial art direction, tactile paper fibers, frosted glass, soft subsurface scattering, restrained fine detail.
>
> Lighting/mood: soft directional museum light, warm highlights, deep olive-gray internal shadows, calm, intelligent, contemplative.
>
> Color palette: warm ivory, parchment, muted sage, oxidized copper, pale lavender-gray, tiny amber light points.
>
> Composition/framing: 16:9 wide, strong depth, foreground and background layers for parallax, no hard frame edges.
>
> Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no UI, no charts, no logos, no people, no watermark.
>
> Avoid: black backgrounds, neon cyberpunk, blue holograms, generic particle clouds, stock neural-network imagery, bright flashes, high contrast strobing.
